Why governance is not optional

ChatAgentAgentic teamDesigned AIGoverned AI

Applies now

EU AI Act: AI literacy obligations apply.

Providers and deployers must support AI literacy among staff and others using AI on their behalf.

AI agents: if an agent qualifies as an AI system under the Act, the applicable rules can apply.

Our governance principle

Every significant AI workflow should have:

  • a human owner
  • clear permissions
  • defined review points
  • accountability for the final output or action

Source: EU AI Act, Arts. 3-4; European Commission, 27 July 2026 · checked 7 October 2026

Accountability & quality checks

If AI tells you it found three important issues in a 200-page policy document, how do you know there were not eight?

Section by section

Ask per chapter, not for the whole document at once.

Page references

Every finding points to where it is.

Ask what it skipped

"Which parts did you not read?"
